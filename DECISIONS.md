# Candidate economic rules

Five rounds and published immutable launch configuration are retained.
Failed R1 makes 100% of original contributed principal refundable, including
previously withheld R1 exit penalties, without paying an earlier refund twice.
Transaction senders pay their own gas. Successful-launch refund penalties enter
the official pool. The mainnet protocol fee is fixed at 2% with reviewed frozen
configuration. All positive final contributions settle before pool creation.
The official LP remains permanently held by the Vault. Creator allocation uses
180-day linear vesting. There is no automatic buyback or LP-recovery mechanism.

Future contract versions apply to new deployments; they do not rewrite existing
launches. See docs/CONTRACTS_TECHNICAL.md for implementation details.
